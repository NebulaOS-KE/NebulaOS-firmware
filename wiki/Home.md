# NebulaOS-firmware

This is where NebulaOS is built. It pins the exact version of every component - the kernel, the
Klipper extensions, NebulaScreen, upstream Klipper, Moonraker, Mainsail, Buildroot - fetches
them fresh, and assembles the complete flashable image. No component repository produces a
working printer image on its own.

This wiki is the **developer** documentation. The end-user manual is the
[NebulaOS wiki](https://github.com/NebulaOS-KE/NebulaOS/wiki).

`main` carries commits that are **not** in 1.0.2. `REPOSITORY_HEAD` is not `SHIPPING_PIN`:
`manifests/dependencies.conf` is the shipping authority, and every page says which of the two it
is describing where the difference bites.

## What this wiki does not cover

An honest list, so nothing looks more finished than it is.

- **Hardware behaviour.** Nothing here was observed on a printer. Endpoints exist, services
  exist and in a known order, and each script's header says what its author says it does - a
  header comment is a description, not an independent analysis.
- **Response shapes for eight API endpoints.** They are composed in the Moonraker component or
  returned by a helper that static analysis cannot read. Those say so on the page instead of
  carrying a plausible example, because to a reader an invented example is indistinguishable
  from a real one.
- **Whether the freeze mitigations work.** [The black box](Black-Box) describes what the image
  does. Nothing here claims the freeze is fixed.
- **Buildroot internals, the filesystem layout, and the kernel's part in the image** - the
  kernel is documented in [its own wiki](https://github.com/NebulaOS-KE/NebulaOS-kernel/wiki).
- **Per-service failure behaviour and dependencies.** [Boot services](Boot-Services) lists all
  36 with each script's stated purpose, not an analysis of each one's failure modes.
- **The roughly 120 design and analysis documents in `docs/`.** They remain the place to read
  *why* things are the way they are, and they are not summarised here. These pages answer *what
  exists*, from source.

Coverage is tracked honestly, per item, in the central repository's
`documentation/agent/COVERAGE.md`.
